# Episode 008: distilled semantic policy, run results

Date: 2026-10-03. Code: `0.jev-starcraft-combat/008-sc2-distilled-semantic-policy/` on `main`
(PR #4). Same scenario as 005-007: 12 Marines vs 10 Banelings + 10 Zerglings, paused, decisions
every 0.54 s, 60 s cap.

## TL;DR

- Behavior cloning works mechanically: the 11-number student predicts the teacher's executed
  action 60% of the time on held-out fights (majority class 32%).
- In the game it wins **16/20**, the same as the 006 semantic net and below the teacher
  (49/50) and `stutter_all` (20/20 in 006). It keeps fewer Marines alive (4.7) and fights longer
  (16.3 s).
- It is cheap and fast: one perception call per decision, median 156 ms, ~157k input tokens per
  fight (~$0.0066), vs the teacher's 497 ms and ~392k tokens.
- The student copies the teacher's action **mix** almost exactly but not its **timing**. It
  never predicts `focus_bane` or `cover_ally` on purpose (0% recall), and stim is effectively
  random.

## 1. Teacher collection

`jev_teacher_collect`, 50 fights, seeds 2000-2049: 49 wins, 1 loss, 7.34 Marines alive, 35.6
survivor HP, 10.25 s. 982 decision rows, 8,860 Marine samples. 0 API errors. Cost $0.82
(commander + soldier calls plus the observing perception call; median 497 ms per decision).

Executed-label counts: attack 2873, stutter 1905, split 1439, focus_bane 880, kite 693,
retreat 669, cover_ally 352, bait 49. Teacher stims on 6.9% of samples.

## 2. Training (`arena train-bc`, 120 epochs, split by episode seed)

| | Action loss | Action acc | Stim loss | Stim acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train (7,130) | 1.088 | 0.593 | 0.227 | 0.931 |
| validation (1,730) | 1.035 | 0.603 | 0.222 | 0.931 |

Majority-class action accuracy is 0.323 (always `attack`). Majority-class stim accuracy is 0.931
(never stim), the same as the stim head: it learned nothing useful. Its highest stim probability
on validation is 0.14 (0.108 mean when the teacher stims, 0.068 when not).

Jev's referential priority Baneling matched the commander's target 97.3% of the time.

## Thoughts and next steps

1. **The bottleneck is the representation, not the learner.** 60% accuracy with near-zero recall
   on the plan-dependent actions means the 11 perceptions don't contain that information.
   Candidate perceptions aimed at the missing classes: "a teammate within 3 cells is low and
   being attacked" (cover_ally), "a Baneling is targeting this Marine" / "this Marine can shoot
   the priority Baneling now" (focus_bane), and a sharper stim cue tied to contact.
2. **Stim:** replace the learned head with a threshold on `stim_opportunity`, or train it with
   class weighting. As is, sampled stim fires at random.
3. **Mixed decoding:** argmax for confident classes (kite, split, attack) and sampling only
   when the top two are close, instead of plain T=1 sampling.
4. **More data is the cheap lever:** 50 fights gave 154 validation focus_bane samples. 100-200
   teacher fights cost about $1.60-3.30 and help only if the features carry the signal (point 1
   first).
5. **Re-run `stutter_all` and `jev_commander_stutter` on seeds 0-19 in the 008 folder** before
   writing it up, per the series rule.
6. RL fine-tuning (007 trainer) only after the student is near the teacher.
