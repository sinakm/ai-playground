# Episode 009: relational semantic graph, run results

Date: 2026-10-04. Code: `0.jev-starcraft-combat/009-sc2-relational-semantic-graph/` on `main`
(PR #6). Same scenario as 005-008: 12 Marines vs 10 Banelings + 10 Zerglings, paused, decisions
every 0.54 s, 60 s cap. All evaluation fights use seeds 0-19 in this folder.

## TL;DR

- **The relational edges fixed the 008 gap.** The distilled student, with no commander, wins
  **20/20** (008: 16/20). The class-balanced student wins 20/20 and the argmax diagnostic 10/10.
- Matched baselines in the same folder: `stutter_all` 20/20 (8.85 alive), `jev_commander_stutter`
  20/20 (7.25 alive). The student now ties both on wins; it keeps fewer Marines alive with sampling
  (6.25) and more with argmax (8.5 on 10 fights).
- New features carry signal: `ally_needs_cover` averages 0.87 on the teacher's `cover_ally` steps
  vs 0.35 on `attack`; `target_engagement_value` is highest on `focus_bane` (0.49).
- **Stim head is miscalibrated:** with the positive-class weight (16.4) every decision gets
  p(stim) about 0.41-0.48, so T=1 sampling stims on 44% of decision steps (teacher 5.7%). It still
  wins, but it costs HP (survivor HP 29.8 vs 34.0 for the commander). Argmax (p >= 0.5) stims on
  6.5% of steps, close to the teacher.
- Student cost per decision is two sequential Jev calls (node + edge stages): median 319 ms,
  ~$0.0115 per fight, vs the commander's 398 ms and ~$0.0105 per fight. No longer cheaper than the
  commander, unlike 008.

## Caveats

- `stutter_all` alone wins 20/20 here, so this scenario can no longer separate the student from
  the commander or from no AI at all on win rate. Marines alive and survivor HP are the
  discriminating metrics, and `stutter_all` is best on both.
- The T=0 row is 10 fights; its 8.5 Marines alive is promising but not yet a 20-fight result.
- The student's 20/20 rests on the code layer inherited from 005 (reflexes, stutter default),
  same as every policy here.
- During the baseline runs the machine slept for several hours. One hung `jev_commander_stutter`
  batch was killed and its incomplete seed re-run; some `stutter_all` batches ran concurrently
  after wake (paused mode, so outcomes are unaffected; latency columns for those are not used).

## Next steps

1. **Fix stim calibration:** threshold at 0.5 even when sampling actions, or drop the positive
   weight and tune a threshold on validation. Expect survivor HP to rise toward the commander's.
2. **Run T=0 for the full 20** and report it next to T=1; it currently looks best among students.
3. **A harder scenario** where `stutter_all` loses (more Banelings, or Zerg that surround), so
   the student's judgment can show up in wins, not just HP.
4. Latency: the two-stage perception is now the cost driver (~320 ms). Batch node and edge
   questions into one call where the target can be referenced implicitly, and compare.

## Round 9b: stim threshold fix (PR #7), 2026-10-04

Same weights (`models/relational_policy.json`), stim now `p(stim) >= 0.5` regardless of action
temperature. 20 fights each, seeds 0-19, isolated run groups (`results/stimfix-t1/`,
`results/argmax20/`).

| Policy | Wins | Marines alive | Survivor HP | Fight s | Median ms | p90 ms | Cost USD | Stim steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stutter_all (9a) | 20/20 | 8.85 | 51.3 | 11.39 | - | - | 0.00 | 0% |
| jev_commander_stutter (9a) | 20/20 | 7.25 | 34.0 | 9.69 | 398.3 | 490.3 | 0.21 | teacher 5.7% |
| student T=1, 9a (sampled stim) | 20/20 | 6.25 | 29.8 | 12.01 | 318.7 | 396.8 | 0.23 | 44.0% |
| **student T=1, 9b (stimfix-t1)** | 20/20 | 6.75 | 30.1 | 13.77 | 321.9 | 409.5 | 0.26 | 7.8% |
| **student T=0, 9b (argmax20)** | 20/20 | **8.95** | 34.2 | 14.87 | 326.0 | 390.0 | 0.32 | 7.6% |

Findings:

- The fix worked mechanically: stim steps dropped from 44% to 7.8% (teacher 5.7%). p(stim) median
  0.42, max 0.52, so stim fires only in the few states that just cross 0.5.
- For T=1 it barely helped: Marines alive 6.25 -> 6.75, survivor HP 29.8 -> 30.1. Random stim was
  not the main cost; the sampled action noise is.
- **Argmax is the best student:** 8.95 Marines alive (minimum 7 in any fight), matching
  `stutter_all` (8.85) and above the commander (7.25). Survivor HP 34.2 equals the commander and is
  well below `stutter_all` (51.3).
- But argmax behaves differently from the teacher: executed attack 55% (teacher 28%), stutter 19%,
  focus_bane 5% (teacher 15%), kite 4%, cover_ally 0%. It collapses toward "attack + stutter",
  which is close to what `stutter_all` does. The rare classes the relational edges unlocked are
  mostly lost at argmax; they only appear when sampling.
- Fights are slower for both students (13.8-14.9 s vs 9.7 s commander, 11.4 s stutter_all).
- Win rate is saturated (everything 20/20). This scenario cannot rank the student above
  `stutter_all`; a harder fight is the next useful experiment.

Suggested next steps: (1) a scenario where `stutter_all` loses (e.g. 14+ Banelings or a surround);
(2) a decoding middle ground: argmax unless the top-2 margin is small, then sample, to keep
focus_bane/cover_ally without the T=1 noise; (3) recalibrate stim (Platt scaling or a validation-tuned
threshold) since 0.5 sits right at the top of its range.
